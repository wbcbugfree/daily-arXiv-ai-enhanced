Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: GACC-Bench 是一个目标自适应上下文压缩基准，包含 500 个高层目标、2,571 个问题、281 个多领域上下文，用于评估 LLM 在给定目标和预算下保留目标相关信息的能力。实验显示十款压缩模型与全上下文仍有显著差距，目标条件一致提升准确率，错配目标则降低性能。

Motivation: 长程 LLM 应用需在下游问题未知前压缩上下文，且必须保留与高层目标相关的信息。现有基准缺乏对“目标自适应”压缩的系统性评估，难以衡量模型按指定目标保留关键信息的可靠性。

Method: 构建 GACC-Bench：从 7 个领域收集 281 个文档与智能体轨迹上下文，将源上下文中的问题按共享下游目标分组得到 500 个高层目标，人工专家审核所有实例。评估时压缩器接收源上下文、目标与长度预算，隐藏评估问题；固定阅读器使用同一压缩上下文独立作答。测试 10 个 LLM 压缩器，对比全上下文、无目标、匹配目标、错配目标四种条件。

Result: 最佳模型仍与全上下文性能存在显著差距，尤其难以保证同一目标下所有问题均答对。目标感知压缩在所有模型上稳定优于无目标压缩；提供错配目标会显著降低准确率，说明目标条件对信息保留有决定性影响。

Conclusion: GACC-Bench 揭示了当前 LLM 在目标自适应上下文压缩上的局限性，确立了目标条件对压缩质量的关键作用，为后续研发目标感知压缩方法提供了标准化评测平台。

Abstract: Context compression in long-running applications of large language models (LLMs) must preserve goal-relevant information, often before specific downstream questions are known. We introduce GACC-Bench, a benchmark for Goal-Adaptive Context Compression that evaluates how reliably LLMs adapt information retention to specified high-level goals. For each source context, we derive one or more high-level goals by grouping source-grounded questions according to shared downstream objectives. The benchmark contains 500 goals and 2,571 questions over 281 document and agent-trace contexts from seven collections spanning diverse domains, with all instances reviewed by human experts. The compressor receives a source context, a goal, and a context-length budget, with the associated evaluation questions withheld. A fixed reader answers these questions individually using the same compressed context. Our evaluation of ten LLM compressors reveals substantial gaps to full-context performance even for the best-performing model, particularly in supporting correct answers to every question associated with a goal. Compared with goal-agnostic compression, target-goal conditioning consistently improves accuracy, whereas mismatched-goal conditioning reduces it. GACC-Bench is available at https://github.com/weiting24/GACC-Bench.
