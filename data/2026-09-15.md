### [1] [The Cost of Compression: A Rate-Distortion Limit on Factual Hallucination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12111)
*Xi Wang,Shijia Xu,Rongfeng Guo*

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 本文提出一个信息论模型，将闭卷问答中的事实幻觉分解为覆盖错误（未观测事实）和压缩错误（有限记忆导致的观测事实有损回忆），并证明了误差下界，通过模拟和语言模型探针验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有观点将事实幻觉主要视为覆盖问题（模型内部记忆缺失相关事实），但忽略了第二个错误来源：即使事实已被观测，有限记忆迫使其只能近似存储，导致压缩失真。

Method: 构建覆盖-压缩模型：无结构问答任务，N个可能查询，K个可能答案。学习者观测M个训练事实，将其压缩至最多B比特，无检索地回答均匀抽取的测试查询。假设真实映射均匀随机，利用零一损失下均匀K元源的逆速率-失真函数δ*(r)，证明误差下界 ℰ ≥ (M/N)δ*(B/M) + (1-M/N)(1-1/K)。两项分别对应观测事实的压缩失真与未观测事实的覆盖缺失。另进行理论指导的模拟和受控事实注入探针实验，变换事实负载和有效可训练记忆。

Result: 证明的界给出了一个紧凑框架，可用于推理选择性记忆、强制压缩、结构、检索、拒答及长上下文组织。实验观察到理论预测的特征：观测事实的有损回忆在有限记忆下是一个可分离的失效模式。

Conclusion: 该结果并非幻觉的完整理论，而是从信息论角度刻画了一个可分离的失效模式：有限记忆下对已观测事实的有损回忆。

This view misses a second source of error. Even when a fact has been observed, finite memory may force it to be stored only approximately. We study this effect through a simple coverage--compression model of factual recall. We consider an unstructured question-answering task with $N$ possible queries and $K$ possible answers. A learner observes $M$ training facts, compresses them into at most $B$ bits, and answers uniformly drawn test queries without retrieval. For a uniformly random ground-truth mapping, we prove $\mathcal{E} \geq \frac{M}{N}δ^\star\!\left(\frac{B}{M}\right) + \left(1-\frac{M}{N}\right)\left(1-\frac{1}{K}\right)$, where $δ^\star(r)$ is the inverse rate-distortion function of a uniform $K$-ary source under zero-one loss. The two terms separate compression distortion on observed facts from missing coverage on unobserved facts. The bound gives a compact way to reason about selective memory, forced compression, structure, retrieval, abstention, and long-context organization. We study the predicted signatures with theory-implied simulations and controlled fact-injection probes in modern language models that vary fact load and effective trainable memory. The result is not a complete theory of hallucination, but an information-theoretic account of a separable failure mode: lossy recall of observed facts under finite memory.

Second, edit effects are composition-sensitive: edits that work in isolation can interfere after composition, causing one or both to lose their benefit or become harmful. ### [76] [Recommendation Retrievers Need Verifiers: Universal Generative Reranking for Sequential Recommendations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12270)
*Benyu Zhang,Qiang Zhang,Rui Li,Qunshu Zhang,Devansh Tandon,Neeraj Bhatia*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 本文提出一种轻量级生成式验证器，用于多阶段推荐系统中的首阶段检索器。验证器通过计算候选物品标识符token的似然来重新评分检索器输出的Top-K候选，无需重新训练检索器，仅在事后用next-token cross entropy训练，无需负采样，推理时仅对检索器的Top-K候选打分。实验表明该方法在多个数据集和检索器模型上稳定提升Recall@10，且消融实验验证了改进并非仅源于引入物品内容特征，而是作为一种事后输出侧适应机制生效。

Motivation: 多阶段推荐系统中，首阶段检索器输出排序候选列表，但仅有限前缀（Top-k）会被传递给下游昂贵的排序阶段。相关物品可能位于检索列表深处而未被包含在实际消费的短前缀中。现有方法通常通过重新训练或替换检索器来提升Recall@k，但成本高且难以适配不同检索器。因此需要一种事后验证机制，在不修改检索器的前提下，将深处的相关物品提升至消费前缀。

Method: 引入轻量级生成式验证器：给定检索器状态（query state）和候选物品，验证器通过计算物品标识符token序列的似然来打分。验证器采用next-token cross entropy进行事后训练，无需负采样或候选池；推理时仅对检索器输出的Top-K候选打分。接口极简：检索器提供query state和候选物品，物品表示可使用任意固定token化方式。验证器作为输出侧适配层，独立于检索器架构。

Conclusion: 生成式验证器提供了一种通用、轻量、无需重训检索器的事后校准方法，能有效将检索列表深处的相关物品提升至消费前缀，显著提升首阶段召回率，且不依赖特定检索器架构或物品内容特征，适用于多阶段推荐系统的实际部署。

Abstract: First-stage recommenders in multi-stage systems produce a ranked candidate list from which a limited prefix is forwarded to downstream rankers. Because each forwarded item must be processed by more expensive ranking stages, this shortlist cannot be arbitrarily large. The first-stage objective is therefore high coverage of relevant items within the forwarded prefix, commonly measured by Recall@$k$. A relevant item may be available deeper in the retrieved list but absent from the shorter prefix that is actually consumed. This paper studies post-hoc verification for promoting such candidates into the consumed shortlist without retraining or replacing the retriever.
  We introduce a lightweight generative verifier for retrieval models. Given a retriever state and a candidate item, the verifier scores the item through the likelihood of its identifier tokens. It is trained post hoc with next-token cross entropy, requires no sampled negatives or candidate pool during training, and scores only the retriever's top-$K$ candidates at inference. The interface is minimal: the retriever supplies a query state and candidate items, and the item representation can use any fixed tokenization.
  Across Amazon product recommendation and YaMBDa music recommendation, the same verifier training recipe improves Recall@10 for SASRec, GRU4Rec, NextItNet, and MiniOneRec. Ablations show that the improvements are not explained solely by injecting item-content features into the retriever, supporting verification as a post-hoc output-side adaptation mechanism.
